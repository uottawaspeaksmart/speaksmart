01 / Who We Are

We believe strong communication is the foundation of academic success, leadership, and professional growth, and that every student deserves the opportunity to develop that strength.

SpeakSmart is a student-led club at the University of Ottawa dedicated to empowering the voices of today’s students. Through interactive workshops, guest speakers, and engaging events, we create a supportive space where students can practice, improve, and discover their voice.

Whether you’re preparing for a class presentation, leading a group project, practicing for an interview, or simply hoping to express your ideas more clearly, this is the space for you. All faculties, all levels of experience.

05 / Our Constitution

Built on principle.

Founded May 9, 2025, SpeakSmart is governed by a ratified constitution. Four values anchor everything we run: every workshop, every debate, every event.

2.2.1

Clarity

Facilitating precise and articulate speech practices.

2.2.2

Confidence

Providing tools and support to improve and strengthen public speaking.

2.2.3

Inclusivity

Ensuring all students, regardless of background, can thrive in the club.

2.2.4

Leadership

Promoting student leadership through speaking opportunities and mentorship.

Art. 5 Membership +

Membership is open and free to all undergraduate students from any faculty or program at the University of Ottawa. You become a member upon registration with SpeakSmart executives, and attending just one event per semester keeps you active.

Art. 7 Events +

Speech Clinics, Debate Nights, Guest Speaker Panels, Networking Seminars, and Confidence Building Workshops. Every event is aligned with the club’s mission and values.

Art. 3 Leadership Team +

An elected executive of eight: President, VP Internal, VP External, VP Communications, VP Francophone Affairs & Equity, VP Events, VP Finance, and VP Marketing. Each serves a one-year term.

Art. 8 Elections +

Executive selection runs annually from late March to late June through a formal application process, open to all registered undergraduate members.

06 / The Team

Eight seats.
One voice.

SpeakSmart is run by an elected executive of eight, each serving a one-year term. Elections open every spring to all registered members. One of these seats could be yours.

3.6.1

President

Oversees all club operations, chairs meetings, represents the club externally, and ensures constitutional compliance.

3.6.2

VP Internal

Maintains documentation, registration lists, club minutes, and internal communications. Signing officer.

3.6.3

VP External

Builds partnerships with organizations and faculties, handles outreach, and supports speaker acquisition.

3.6.4

VP Communications

Develops content for social media, marketing materials, club branding, and newsletters.

3.6.5

VP Francophone Affairs & Equity

Provides bilingual content and French programming while keeping every event inclusive, safe, and accessible.

3.6.6

VP Events

Coordinates all event logistics, speaker sessions, debates, and skill-building workshops.

3.6.7

VP Finance

Manages budgets, funding proposals, and club expenses. Signing officer.

3.6.8

VP Marketing

Oversees promotion and branding, coordinates event marketing, and works to grow membership and engagement.

Art. 8

This could be you

Executive selection runs every spring, open to all registered members. Watch for applications between March and June.
